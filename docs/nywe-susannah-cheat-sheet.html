Your 5-step workflow

  1. Home — Blue box? Click Review now and Approve the PDF.
  2. Exhibitor list — Check wineries marked Not in system → Create drafts.
  3. Approve — Open each license, check PDF, click Approve.
  4. Send — Filter Approved → select wineries → Send selected (DocuSign emails them).
  5. Sign — When they sign, open your DocuSign email and countersign. Accounting is notified automatically.
